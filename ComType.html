🎮 How to Play

Follow these 5 simple steps and start your typing quest!

1
Start a gameChoose your difficulty, then press the Single Player button.
2
Listen carefullyPress the large speaker button and listen to the spoken word.
3
Type what you hearEnter the word in the typing box, then press Enter or Submit.
4
Replay when neededPress 🔊 Replay or use the ↑ Up Arrow key to hear the word again.
5
Earn points and beat the clockYou get one retry for each word. Type accurately and finish before time runs out!
💡 Helpful tip: Use headphones and listen to the whole word before typing.
