How to Play

  1. ListenPress Play Audio and listen closely to the word or sentence.
  2. ThinkTake a beat — you can replay the audio as many times as you need.
  3. TypeType exactly what you heard into the input box, including punctuation.
  4. SubmitPress Enter or tap Submit to check your answer.
  5. FeedbackSee if you were correct, keep your combo alive, and move to the next challenge.

Tip: accuracy and combo matter as much as speed — a clean streak scores higher than a fast, sloppy one.
